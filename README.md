# An-Overview-on-PCR-Polymerase-Chain-Reaction-_A-Seminar-Paper-by-Fatima-Mostofa_
This seminar paper explores the fundamentals and applications of Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR), a vital technique used in molecular biology to amplify DNA sequences.

Objective :  
Submitted as part of my undergraduate coursework in the **Department of Biotechnology & Genetic Engineering** at **Sylhet Agricultural University**, this paper aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of PCR and its significance in life science research.

Topics Covered  
- Historical background & invention  
- PCR cycle: Denaturation, Annealing, Extension  
- Equipment: Thermal cycler  
- Types of PCR: RT-PCR, qPCR, Multiplex, etc.  
- Applications: Disease diagnosis, agriculture, forensic science
